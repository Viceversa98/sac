## Suggested rewrites for the proposal (drop-in)

- **Market scale box:** "Anchored by Indonesia's Nile tilapia (*Oreochromis niloticus*) sector, the country's largest farmed fish by volume at 1.37 million tonnes in 2023 (KKP Satu Data, via SEAFDEC 2025), produced by an aquaculture base of ~940,000 fish-farming households (BPS Agricultural Census 2023)."
  - Alternative, if you confirm the KKP portal while logged in: "…1.56 million tonnes of grow-out Nile tilapia in 2024 (KKP Satu Data)." Do not cite BPS for this number.
- **Headline figure:** change "1.56M+ Tons" to "1.37M Tons (2023)". If the portal is confirmed, use "1.56M Tons (2024, KKP)".
- **Opportunities bullet:** "Automates biochemical calculations (C/N ratio, molasses dosing, daily feed rations) to reduce the manual-management errors that peer-reviewed reviews identify as a leading cause of biofloc failure (Khanjani et al., 2024), and optimises feed, the single largest cost in biofloc tilapia (58% of production cost in a Thai case study; Tongsiri et al., 2020)."
- **USP "Zero-Hallucination":** rename to "**Source-Grounded Biofloc Reasoning**: every chemical and feeding recommendation is generated from a curated RAG knowledge base (Avnimelech 1999; Pérez-Fuentes et al. 2016; SDSU tilapia water-quality limits), shows its citation, enforces hard-coded safety limits, and requires supervisor approval before execution."
- **VFD line:** "VFD (0.75 kW) aerator drive: ~$50–160. DO-driven speed control cut aeration energy by 25% in a UF/IFAS trial."
- **DO sensor line:** "Maker-grade galvanic DO sensor kit ~$170–215 (DFRobot / Atlas Scientific) + membrane and electrolyte consumables."
- **Multi-parameter line:** "pH (lab/industrial) + TDS + temperature: ~$59–84."
- **ESP32 line:** "ESP32 with isolated RS485: ~$19–37."
- **BOM total:** "~$340–460 per pond (list prices, before shipping and duty; local sourcing may lower this)." Recompute IDR with a dated exchange rate.
- **Cloud SQL:** "Cloud SQL for MySQL (db-f1-micro, shared core, no SLA): ~$8/month compute + storage ≈ $10–15/month." Remove "High-availability" or budget about $15+/month extra for HA.
- **Gemini line:** "Gemini API pay-as-you-go: e.g., Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite at $0.25 per 1M input / $1.50 per 1M output tokens (Google, Oct 2026). Free tier available for prototyping."
